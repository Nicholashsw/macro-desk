Privacy Policy

Macro Desk · effective 19 October 2026

What Macro Desk is

Macro Desk answers questions about published US economic statistics, such as inflation, employment and Treasury yields. It has no user accounts and does not ask for, receive or store information about who you are.

What we receive

When you ask Alexa a question that Macro Desk handles, Alexa sends us the name of the tool to run and its parameters, for example the indicator you asked about. We do not receive your voice recording, your Amazon account details, your contacts, your location or any device identifier.

What we keep

Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs (time, requested path, response status, and the network address of the calling service) for up to 30 days to operate and secure the service. We do not build profiles, sell data, or share it with anyone except as required by law.

Amazon

Your conversation with Alexa is handled by Amazon under the Amazon Privacy Notice.
